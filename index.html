First-Year Teacher Creating a Science Lesson Slide Deck on Canva for Education
👩‍🏫

Ms. Tran

1st-year 5th grade science teacher
Comfortable with Google Slides, new to Canva
School recently adopted Canva for Education
Needs to create visually engaging lessons to keep students focused

Scenario

Ms. Tran's school has adopted Canva for Education. She needs to create an engaging slide deck for a 5th grade lesson on the water cycle. She wants to use Canva's education templates and share the finished deck with students through Google Classroom, all within her 50-minute planning period.

User Goals & Expectations

• Find a science-specific template quickly
• Customize it for her water cycle lesson in under 30 min
• Make it visually appealing without design skills
• Share directly to Google Classroom
• Reuse or adapt it for future lessons

🪦 Revised Assumptions

  • Education templates would be organized by subject and grade
  • Google Classroom integration would be one-click
  • All template elements would be fully editable
  • The "Magic Design" AI would understand educational content needs

Section 3: Analysis

Opportunities & Insights

✓ What's Working
SSO login and polished dashboard create a strong first impression. Templates are visually far superior to Google Slides defaults.
Stage: Get Started
! Friction Point
Search mixes all content types (presentations, posters, worksheets) together. 200+ results with no grade-level or subject filter creates decision fatigue.
Stage: Find a Template
Heuristic #8: Minimalist Design
! Friction Point
Grouped template elements prevent easy editing. Teacher expects to swap a diagram but can't access individual components without ungrouping — a hidden advanced action.
Stage: Customize
Heuristic #6: Recognition over Recall
! Friction Point
"Magic Design" AI generates business/marketing content, not education-specific layouts. Mismatch between tool capability and teacher's context.
Stage: Customize
Heuristic #2: Match Real World
✓ What's Working
Final product quality exceeds what teacher could create in Google Slides. Visual appeal directly supports student engagement with science content.
Stage: Reflect
💡 Opportunity
Add an "Education" filter that organizes templates by grade band and subject area. Surface curriculum-aligned templates first for edu accounts.
Stage: Find a Template
💡 Opportunity
Train Magic Design AI on education use cases so it generates lesson-appropriate layouts rather than defaulting to business/marketing templates.
Stage: Customize
! Friction Point
Google Classroom integration hidden under "More" in share menu. For an education-specific product, LMS sharing should be a primary action, not buried.
Stage: Share
Heuristic #6: Recognition over Recall
? Open Questions
Do teachers create a second presentation? What's the return rate? Does the time investment decrease significantly for repeat users?
Stage: Reflect
$ Learning Impact
If "Save as Template" was prompted after first creation, teacher's second deck could take 15 min instead of 50 — a 70% time savings that makes the tool sustainable.
